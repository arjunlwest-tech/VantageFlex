📋 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Vantage Flex respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our fitness platform. We collect minimal data and never sell your personal information to third parties.

2. Data We Collect

Local Storage Only

All your data—workouts, recipes, meal plans, and preferences—is stored locally in your browser. We don't have access to your personal fitness data.

Anonymous Usage

We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app. This includes feature usage counts and error reports—never personal information.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for app functionality. Third-party cookies may be used for analytics and advertising through Google services.

3. Your Control

  • ✓ Clear all data anytime from your browser settings
  • ✓ Export your workout history (feature coming soon)
  • ✓ No account required—use anonymously
  • ✓ Opt out of analytics in your browser settings

4. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to support the platform. Google may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your visit. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at privacy@vantagesuites.com
